Saffron Street

Delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your Saffron Street: Merge & Serve game account and the data linked to it. Saffron Street is made by TonTech.

Option 1: in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Saffron Street.
  2. Go to Settings → Friends → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and the game starts fresh on this device.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the game any more, email info@tontech.co with the subject Saffron Street account deletion and include your friend code (8 letters and digits, shown in Settings → Friends and in any invite link you shared, for example .../i/ABCDEFGH). We don't collect your name or email in the game, so the friend code is how we find your account. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your game account and the device sign-in ID linked to it.
  • Your cloud save (game progress), profile, level and friend code.
  • Your friends list, blocks and invite links, and any pending rewards.
  • The game's data on your device, when you delete in the game.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Reports about other players that you made, or that were made about you, are kept for moderation with your account ID removed, so they no longer identify you.
  • Purchase records (if you ever made a purchase) are kept without your account ID, to prevent refund fraud and meet accounting rules.
  • Server backups may hold a copy of your data for up to 14 days after deletion, after which they are overwritten.
  • Install attribution: the install report sent to our attribution partner NUVIAD (advertising ID, if you allowed tracking, and a random install ID) is not linked to your game account. To stop future use, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
