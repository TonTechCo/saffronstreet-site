Saffron Street Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-17

This policy explains what information Saffron Street: Merge & Serve (the "game", operated by TonTech) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. By using the game you agree to this policy.

1. The short version

  • You can play without an email, phone number or real name.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • If you allow tracking, your advertising ID is used for install attribution (section 3).
  • The game has no ads and no in-app purchases yet. We will update this policy before either launches.
  • You can delete your game account in the game: Settings → Friends → Delete account.

2. Who we are

Saffron Street is a cooking and merge game published by TonTech. For privacy questions, see section 10.

3. Information we collect

Advertising identifier and install attribution

The advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Google advertising ID on Android) is read only if you allow tracking: on iOS after you accept Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and on Android when ad tracking is allowed on your device. It is sent, together with a random per-install identifier, to NUVIAD (notify.nuviad.com) so we can measure which campaigns led to an install. On iOS, Apple's SKAdNetwork also sends privacy-preserving install postbacks directly from Apple; these do not come from the game. If you decline tracking or limit ad tracking, no advertising ID is sent. Like any server, NUVIAD sees the IP address of the request; we do not use it to work out your location.

Game account on our server

To power friends and cloud saves, the game creates an account on TonTech's server at street-api.tontech.co. The account uses a device-generated identifier, not your email or phone number. The server may store:

  • the device-generated account identifier;
  • an optional display name (moderated; shown to friends), plus your avatar, level and chef title;
  • your friend code, your friends list, and invites you send or accept;
  • your cloud save (game progress);
  • reports and blocks you submit about other players, and reports made about you;
  • push notification tokens, once remote notifications are added (not in use yet).

The game has no text chat. Players interact with preset emotes only.

Local notifications

The game can schedule reminders (for example when energy is full) on your device. These are local notifications: they are created and stored on your device and collect nothing. You can turn them off in the game or in your device settings.

Location, contacts, photos, microphone

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone audio, and we do not knowingly collect your real name, email address or phone number.

Crash reporting

Crash reporting (Sentry) is not active in the game today. If we turn it on, crash reports will contain technical diagnostics (device model, OS version, error details), not your identity, and we will update this policy first.

4. Advertising and purchases: not yet

The game currently shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. We plan to add them. Before they launch, we will update this policy to describe the ad partners, the data they use and how purchases are processed, and we will change the effective date above.

5. How we use information

  • To run the game: keep your cloud save, show your profile to friends and handle invites.
  • To keep the community safe: handle reports and blocks and moderate display names.
  • To measure whether our marketing leads to installs (advertising ID, only with your permission).

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Sharing

  • NUVIAD: advertising ID and per-install identifier for install attribution (section 3).
  • Apple: App Store services and SKAdNetwork postbacks. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Other players: your display name, avatar, level, chef title and friend code are visible to players you connect with.
  • TonTech's server (street-api.tontech.co) stores the game account data in section 3.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Tracking: decline the ATT prompt or change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete your account: in the game, open Settings → Friends → Delete account. This removes your friend code, invites, friendships, cloud save, profile and the account itself. Reports you made are anonymised so that moderation records stay intact. If you can't open the game, see Delete your account.
  • Local data: progress is also stored on your device; uninstalling the game removes it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us to exercise them.

8. Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Cloud saves and friend data are deleted with the account. Server backups may keep a copy for up to 14 days after deletion before they are overwritten.

9. Children

Saffron Street is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact info@tontech.co.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted here with a new effective date, and we will update it before adding ads, in-app purchases or crash reporting.
