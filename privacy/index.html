Saffron Street Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-18

This policy explains what information Saffron Street: Merge & Serve (the "game", operated by TonTech) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. By using the game you agree to this policy.

1. The short version

  • You can play without an email, phone number or real name.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • If you allow tracking, your advertising ID is used for install attribution (section 3).
  • The game sends usage statistics to our own server so we can improve it. They are never used for ads and never shared, and you can turn them off in Settings → Usage statistics.
  • The game has no ads and no in-app purchases yet. We will update this policy before either launches.
  • You can delete your game account in the game: Settings → Account → Delete account.

2. Who we are

Saffron Street is a cooking and merge game published by TonTech. For privacy questions, see section 10.

3. Information we collect

Advertising identifier and install attribution

The advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Google advertising ID on Android) is read only if you allow tracking: on iOS after you accept Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and on Android when ad tracking is allowed on your device. It is sent, together with a random per-install identifier, to NUVIAD (notify.nuviad.com) so we can measure which campaigns led to an install. On iOS, Apple's SKAdNetwork also sends privacy-preserving install postbacks directly from Apple; these do not come from the game. If you decline tracking or limit ad tracking, no advertising ID is sent. Like any server, NUVIAD sees the IP address of the request; we do not use it to work out your location.

Game account on our server

To power friends and cloud saves, the game creates an account on TonTech's server at street-api.tontech.co. The account uses a device-generated identifier, not your email or phone number. The server may store:

  • the device-generated account identifier;
  • an optional display name (moderated; shown to friends), plus your avatar, level and chef title;
  • your friend code, your friends list, and invites you send or accept;
  • your cloud save (game progress);
  • reports and blocks you submit about other players, and reports made about you;
  • if you choose Sign in with Apple (iOS) or Sign in with Google (Android) to save your progress: the account identifier Apple or Google provides for this app. We do not ask Apple or Google for your name or email address;
  • push notification tokens (Apple Push Notification service, Firebase Cloud Messaging), only while you allow notifications and keep reminders on, used to tell you about challenge results, your Partner Week partner and gifts. They are removed when you turn reminders off;
  • your social play: Recipe Challenges you send or play, your Partner Week pairing, progress and gifts, and your Cook-Off rating and match results;
  • recorded runs: when you finish a rated Cook-Off, the moves of that shift and its score are kept and may be replayed to another player as a clearly labelled recorded run with your display name. Recipe Challenges likewise show your recorded run to the friend you challenged.

The game has no text chat. Players interact with preset emotes only.

Usage statistics (first-party analytics)

To understand how the game is played and improve it, the game records gameplay events and sends them to TonTech's own game server (no third-party analytics service). Examples: the tutorial steps you complete, story episodes started and finished, totals of merges, dishes and orders (counted, not recorded one by one), Service Shift results, level-ups, running out of energy, shop visits and purchase attempts, ads offered, watched or failed, invites sent or accepted, friends added, and settings you change (such as the language).

Each event carries a random session identifier, the time, the app version, the platform (iOS or Android) and the game language, and it is stored with your game account identifier. Usage statistics do not include your advertising ID, any device identifier, your location or your name, and we do not store your IP address with them. We use them only in aggregate (for example how many players return the next day, or finish the tutorial) and never to target ads or build advertising profiles.

We rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time: turn off Settings → Usage statistics and the game stops collecting them and deletes any statistics it has not sent yet.

Local notifications

The game can schedule reminders (for example when energy is full) on your device. These are local notifications: they are created and stored on your device and collect nothing. You can turn them off in the game or in your device settings.

Location, contacts, photos, microphone

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone audio, and we do not knowingly collect your real name, email address or phone number.

Crash reporting

Crash reporting (Sentry) is not active in the game today. If we turn it on, crash reports will contain technical diagnostics (device model, OS version, error details), not your identity, and we will update this policy first.

4. Purchases and advertising

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. To deliver what you bought and prevent fraud, our server checks each purchase with Apple or Google and stores the transaction identifier, the product, the purchase date and your game account identifier.

Advertising: the public version of the game shows no ads today. Before ads launch, we will update this policy to describe the ad partner (Google AdMob), the consent choices you will have and the data it uses, and we will change the effective date above.

5. How we use information

  • To run the game: keep your cloud save, show your profile to friends and handle invites.
  • To keep the community safe: handle reports and blocks and moderate display names.
  • To measure whether our marketing leads to installs (advertising ID, only with your permission).
  • To understand and improve the game: aggregate usage statistics such as retention, tutorial completion and feature use (section 3).

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Sharing

  • NUVIAD: advertising ID and per-install identifier for install attribution (section 3).
  • Apple: App Store services, purchase verification, Sign in with Apple, push delivery and SKAdNetwork postbacks. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google (Android): Google Play purchase verification, Sign in with Google, Block Store (to keep your game account across reinstalls) and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push delivery. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Other players: your display name, avatar, level, chef title and friend code are visible to players you connect with. Your display name and Cook-Off rating are shown to opponents and Partner Week partners, and recorded runs are shown as described in section 3.
  • TonTech's server (street-api.tontech.co) stores the game account data and usage statistics in section 3. Usage statistics are not shared with anyone.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Tracking: decline the ATT prompt or change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete your account: in the game, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your friend code, invites, friendships, cloud save, profile, usage statistics and the account itself. Reports you made are anonymised so that moderation records stay intact. If you can't open the game, see Delete your account.
  • Usage statistics: turn them off in Settings → Usage statistics. Statistics not yet sent are deleted on the device; those already sent are deleted with your account, or after 13 months.
  • Local data: progress is also stored on your device; uninstalling the game removes it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us to exercise them.

8. Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Cloud saves, friend data and usage statistics are deleted with the account. Usage statistics are also deleted automatically 13 months after they were recorded. Server backups may keep a copy for up to 14 days after deletion before they are overwritten.

9. Children

Saffron Street is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact info@tontech.co.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted here with a new effective date, and we will update it before adding ads or crash reporting.
