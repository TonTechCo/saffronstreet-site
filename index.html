Saffron Street was once the tastiest street in town. Now the shutters are down, the ovens are cold, and the keys to Hettie's Bakery are yours.

Merge, combine, cook

  • Tap the pantry, the cooler and the orchard for fresh ingredients.
  • Merge two of the same to make something better: flour, sugar, berries, butter and more.
  • Combine different ingredients into real recipes, then cook them in the oven or on the stove.
  • Tap when the ring glows gold for a perfect dish. Miss it and it's still delicious. Nothing ever burns.

Serve the counter, when you feel like it

  • Open a Service Shift for a lively two minutes of happy customers, combos and tips.
  • Shifts are always optional. The board is calm, and every chapter can be finished without them.
  • Prefer it gentler? Relaxed mode gives customers extra patience.

Bring the street back to life

  • Renovate a bakery, a pizzeria, a ramen bar and a taqueria, room by room.
  • Pick the decor you love, and change it any time for free.
  • Follow eight chapters of warm, funny story with Gran Ida, rival chef Celeste, the disguise-loving critic Hugo, courier Juno and a mysterious friend of the street.

Cosy by design

  • Generous energy and daily gifts.
  • Play offline, anywhere.
  • A recipe book that fills up as you cook. No duplicate cards to chase.

Pull up a chair. The street is waiting.
